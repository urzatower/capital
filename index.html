About

Urza Tower Capital is a personal, self-funded book of sealed Magic: The Gathering and other sealed product (booster boxes, collector displays, vintage packs, decks and bundles), run as a fund: every acquisition is recorded in a provenance-stamped ledger at its all-in order cost (after discount and tax), and every position is marked against public marketplace prices. Sealed product has a supply schedule that ends when the print run ends, and available float shrinks each time a box is opened. The book exists to hold that scarcity, not to trade it.

Strategy

Focus areas

  • Crossover sets with an audience outside the game.
  • Vintage and legacy sealed: out-of-print packs, tournament packs, starter and theme decks.
  • Small, dated entries rather than single large allocations.

Approach

  • 100% sealed inventory. Nothing gets opened.
  • Marks from public retail and marketplace price data, never from asking prices alone.
  • No leverage, no borrowed capital, no obligation to sell.
  • Reprint risk is the main hazard and is tracked per set.
