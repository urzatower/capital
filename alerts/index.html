Faller rule: BUY only when the landed entry price is at least 10 percent below the TCGplayer market price, the same untested rule; a fall alone never qualifies. NEAR: a row that is not a BUY but whose landed entry price is within 10 percent of that level (landed at or below the TCGplayer market price for fallers and store gaps, a 13-week margin between -5 points and zero for breakouts); the NEAR band is a default and untested.
