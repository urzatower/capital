Faller rule: BUY only when the landed entry price is at least 10 percent below the TCGplayer market price, the same untested rule; a fall alone never qualifies. NEAR: a row that is not a BUY but whose landed entry price is within 10 percent of that level (landed at or below the TCGplayer market price for fallers and store gaps, a 13-week margin between -5 points and zero for breakouts); the NEAR band is a default and untested. Every other row is WATCH. The gap to buy column is the percent the landed entry price would have to fall to become a BUY.

A store gap says one store lists a product for less than another today, so it marks a cheaper place to acquire it, not a round trip. Card Kingdom's buylist price is a median 70 percent of the cheaper landed price (6 of 15 gaps have one), and the price after a 13 percent marketplace fee at the other store's listed price is a median +3.4 percent from the cheaper landed price, so a negative figure is a shortfall.

Landed price is the price times (1 + 7 percent sales tax) plus shipping (Card Kingdom $13.15, free from n/a; TCGplayer $5.00, free from $50.00).
