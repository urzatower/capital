The book in one paragraph

Urza Tower Capital LP is a personal, self-funded book of sealed trading card product held for scarcity rather than traded. Every acquisition is recorded at its all-in order cost after discount and tax, and every position is marked daily against public marketplace prices. Nothing is opened, nothing is levered, and no position has been sold since inception on 2024-11-01.

Why sealed

Print runs end. Each box that is opened leaves the float and does not come back, so supply falls while the audience for a set widens through films, games and reprint-free formats. The book exists to hold that scarcity through the reprint cycle, funded only by its own capital.

How positions are chosen

  • Crossover sets with an audience outside the game, where demand is not only from players.
  • Vintage and legacy sealed, out-of-print packs, tournament packs, starter and theme decks.
  • Small, dated entries rather than single large allocations, so the entry price is an average.

How risk is controlled

  • 100% sealed inventory. Nothing is opened, so condition risk stays at grading rather than contents.
  • Reprint risk is the main hazard and is tracked per set.
  • No leverage and no redemption pressure, so a drawdown is never a forced seller.

Valuation policy

  • A position is marked only from the transaction-derived market price for its product, latest observation per item, from the same public feed that builds the benchmark.
  • Dealer retail and dealer buylist are an ask and a bid, not a market price, and are never substituted.
  • A product the feed does not cover is carried at cost and flagged, never estimated. Today that is 13 of 51 positions, 8.7% of cost basis.
  • Where a product has no sale history at all, such as a presale, the position may be marked to the lowest public ask and is labelled as an ask in the ledger.

Why this benchmark

A book of sealed product has no published index to be measured against, so the benchmark is built from the same price source that marks the book: every Magic booster box the feed covers, value-weighted with a cap so no single box drives the index, chain-linked across the boxes priced at both ends of each step.

It answers the only question that matters for this strategy: would simply holding the market in sealed boxes have done better. The book is measured against it in every monthly table and in the chart on the performance page.

Limits

  • Ask-marked and uncovered positions are in the book but not in the index.
  • The index is price only. It has no fees, no shipping and no tax, while the book's cost basis is all-in.

Risks and disclosures

What can go wrong

Principal risks

  • Reprint risk. A reprint of a set or a card inside it can reset the price of sealed product. This is the main hazard in the book and is tracked per set.
  • Concentration. The largest bucket is LOTR: Tales of Middle-earth at 45.0% of marked value, and the largest position is The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-… at 40.5%.
  • Liquidity. Sealed product trades in a retail marketplace. Exiting a large box at the marked price is not guaranteed, and shipping, fees and grading all cost money on the way out.
  • Marking. 13 positions are carried at cost and one product is marked to an ask. Those marks are not transaction prices.
  • Physical risk. Condition, storage, theft and damage. The book holds sealed goods, not securities.
  • Single-publisher risk. Most of the book depends on decisions taken by one publisher.

Important information

This document describes a personal collection tracked with fund discipline. It is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, interest or product, and it is not investment advice. Nothing here is an invitation to invest, and no interests are available.

Past performance is not a guide to future results. Returns shown are unrealised, calculated from public marketplace prices, and no position has been sold. Marks can move sharply, and the value of sealed product can fall as well as rise.
